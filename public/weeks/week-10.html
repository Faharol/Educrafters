Timing plan

A timing plan for 45 minutes

Five minutes of planning saves fifteen minutes of rewriting.

  1. 0 to 5 min · Read

    Read the situation twice. Underline the target. Read Figure 1 with the questions from the task format. Read Table 1 against the target.

  2. 5 to 10 min · Plan

    Plan on the question paper: three findings sentences, one or two measures, the numbers you will use.

  3. 10 to 38 min · Write

    Introduction (two sentences). Findings (three patterns). Recommendation (who, what, when, condition, urgency).

  4. 38 to 45 min · Check

    Count words. Check every number against the visuals. Check hedging, parallel lists, tense. Sign the cover.

Avoid Common errors

Six errors that cost the most marks

01

Listing the data

“January 920, February 950, March 970…” is a table in sentences. Group, compare, calculate.

02

Recommending in the findings

“…which is why the company should…” Findings describe. Save the verdict for the last paragraph.

03

Ignoring the table

A recommendation that names no cost, saving or time has not used Table 1. That is a criterion lost.

04

Overclaiming

“This will solve the problem.” The table says “estimated”. Your verbs must say “could”, “is expected to”, “up to”.

05

Wrong length

Under 220 means something is missing. Over 260 means something is padding. Both are penalised.

06

Personal pronouns

“I think we should…” is a message, not a report. “It is recommended that…” is the register.

After the test: Poster Supervision 2

The outline and draft check, in the remaining class time.

Poster Supervision 2

What happens in Supervision 2

  1. Ten minutes per group

    The leader presents the current draft on a laptop or printout. The supervisor asks three questions and gives three actions.

  2. What is checked

    Structure against the six sections. Three problems and three solutions, each with a source. Reading path. Text-to-graphics balance.

  3. What is not checked yet

    Colours, fonts and final wording. That is Week 12. Today is about whether the argument stands.

  4. Outcome

    A written list of actions in your group folder. Supervision 2 is formative: no marks, but the actions are checked at submission.

  • Three solutions compared on the same three criteria in one table.
  • At least one chart or diagram you made yourselves, with a caption.
