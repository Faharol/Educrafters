From the Scheme of Work

How to score well.

01

Weeks 1–4 build the Reading Test

Section B tests the Week 1–2 theory, so keep those notes.

02

The poster is a marathon

Brief (Wk 5) → Supervision 1 (Wk 6) → Supervision 2 (Wk 10) → submit (Wk 12). Bring something to show at every checkpoint.

03

Writing Test = a report from a chart

Practise the three-part structure in Weeks 5, 7 and 9 until 45 minutes feels comfortable.

04

Speaking is 30%

Week 11 teaches the phrases for leading, agreeing, disagreeing and deciding. Week 12 is your mock run.

Attendance matters: face-to-face weeks (3, 5, 6, 8, 10, 12, 13, 14) include supervisions and assessments you cannot easily make up.
