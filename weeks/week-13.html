What it measures. CO3: presenting sustained arguments and engaging critically in formal discussion on technical topics. Two-task ELHAM format, CEFR B2 to C1 descriptors (ELHAM Table 5.4), every criterion out of 5.

Public holiday: Wednesday 6 January is Israk dan Mikraj. Check the schedule for your group's exact slot.

Exam flow

How the session runs

Four candidates per group. About 35 minutes from sign-in to release.

  1. Report. Arrive 20 minutes before your slot. Sign in. Phones off and handed to the invigilator.

  2. Task 1 prep. Receive the scenario and your assigned option. Two minutes. Notes on the card provided.

  3. Task 1. Present for two to three minutes. The examiner will stop you at three.

  4. Task 2 prep. Three minutes with your group. Agree an order of business.

  5. Task 2. Twelve minutes. Reach a decision. The examiner does not intervene.

Task 1 · 10% Individual presentation · 2–3 min

Task 1: individual presentation

01

You receive

A technical scenario, a target audience (for example the operations manager) and one assigned option with its figures.

02

You deliver

Context and hook (30 s). Core proposal with evidence (90 s). Benefits and call to action (30 s).

03

You are scored on

Grammatical accuracy, lexical range and register, fluency and pronunciation. Each out of 5.

04

Remember

Notes are for glancing, not reading. Say the numbers. Hedge the estimates. Finish with a request, not a fade.

Rehearse Task 1 · real timings

Run Task 1 against the clock

Use any option from the Week 12 mock prompts, or a technical problem from your own field. Run the timer and speak out loud.

  • I glanced at my notes; I did not read them.
  • I said the key numbers out loud.
  • I hedged my estimates (for example “approximately”, “we expect”).
  • I finished with a request, not a fade.
  • I finished before the three-minute stop.
Task 2 · 20% Group discussion · 12 min

Task 2: group discussion

01

You receive

A shared board-level decision: choose one measure within a budget. Each candidate defends the option from Task 1, then the group decides.

02

You must

Open, present, compare, challenge, summarise and decide. Someone must call time. Everyone must speak.

03

You are scored on

All four criteria, including interactive effectiveness: turn-taking, responding, building on and challenging points, driving to a decision.

04

Remember

Agreeing with everyone scores as silence. Disagreeing with a reason and a figure scores as C1.
